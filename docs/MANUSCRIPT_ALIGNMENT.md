# Manuscript alignment

Associated manuscript title:

**Three decades of mangrove persistence and turnover in Sulu, Philippines: Cross-product uncertainty and interpretable spatial modelling for management prioritization**

Target journal: **Journal of Environmental Management**.

The repository uses the same interpretive safeguards as the manuscript:

- the study is framed as an environmental-management workflow rather than a simple mapping case study;
- cross-product consensus is a conservative evidence tier;
- annual product disagreement is treated as management-relevant uncertainty;
- interpretable machine learning is used to characterize environmental correlates, not to infer causal drivers;
- spatial cross-validation is primary because adjacent coastal pixels are autocorrelated;
- screening outputs direct field verification rather than automatic intervention.
